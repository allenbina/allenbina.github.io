I've been playing PC games forever. But there was a period where I got genuinely competitive.

## Overwatch

Peak Masters 1 as DPS. Diamond 4 as support, but I wasn't trying as hard on that role. The grind to Masters was real — the kind of thing where you're reviewing VODs, thinking about positioning, and getting tilted at teammates who won't group up. Classic Overwatch experience.

## Hearthstone

This is where I went deep. Hit top 500 legend a couple of times. Peak was top 200. At that level you're playing against streamers and people who do this for a living. Every decision matters — you're counting cards, tracking probabilities, planning three turns ahead.

Eventually I chilled out. Stopped grinding meta decks and started playing fun decks instead. Still hit Masters every season without really trying, which honestly felt better than the sweaty top 200 pushes. Turns out the game is more fun when you're not treating it like a second job.
