My friend was about to throw this speaker away. It wouldn't charge and the sound was cutting in and out. Before he tossed it I asked if I could take a look.

Cracked it open and found two problems. A wire had come unsoldered from the driver — probably from being dropped. That explained the intermittent audio. The USB charging port was physically loose, wobbling in its housing. The solder joints on the port's mounting pins had cracked, so it would only charge if you held the cable at exactly the right angle.

The wire was a quick reflow. The USB port needed a bit more work — I resoldered the pins and then hit the whole thing with epoxy to lock it in place permanently. No more wobble.

Thirty minutes of work. Speaker's still going.
